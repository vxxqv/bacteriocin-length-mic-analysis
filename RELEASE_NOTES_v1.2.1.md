# Bacteriocin protein architecture and MIC analysis v1.2.1

This release corrects the compartment layout of the original protein bacteriocin mechanism figure. The dataset, statistical analyses, and reported numerical results are unchanged.

## Figure correction

- Peptidoglycan damage is shown in the periplasm.
- Pore formation is shown at the inner membrane.
- DNA or RNA cleavage is shown in the cytoplasm.
- Ton- and Tol-linked import are presented as alternative systems.
- The figure now states that functional-module order varies among bacteriocin families.

The editable PowerPoint source and final PNG are included under `analysis_package/figures/publication`.
