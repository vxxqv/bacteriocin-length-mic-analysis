# Bacteriocin protein architecture and MIC analysis v1.1.1

This final reproducibility release contains 47 source-defined MIC observations from 22 bacteriocins across nine primary studies, four protein families, and six assay groups.

The workflow preserves exact, bounded, and right-censored MIC measurements and combines official IUPred3, InterProScan, and Clustal Omega outputs with interval regression, protein-cluster bootstrapping, false-discovery-rate control, robustness analyses, and held-out-protein prediction.

Four of six prespecified features met false-discovery-rate control in pooled models. Greater total length, global disorder, N-terminal disorder, and functional-domain charge were associated with lower MIC. Family, assay, and study analyses define these as testable cross-family signals rather than established independent predictors.

The release includes the complete analysis dataset, validated accessions and sequences, source ledger, code, model outputs, provenance, figures, and supplementary tables.
